A tiny skater on your windows

DeskSkate lives in your menu bar. He rolls along the tops of your windows, rides them when you drag them, and goes flying when you flick one up.

Rides your windows

He ollies up onto higher windows and drops off the ends. Drag a window and he rides along. Flick it up and stop, and he keeps going.

Pick him up

Grab him with the mouse and he dangles, legs pedalling. Let go over a window to drop him on it, or swing and throw.

Ramps and rails

Add a quarter pipe, kicker, rail or box from the menu bar and drag it anywhere. Right-click to flip or remove. They stay put between launches.

Watch him, or drive

By default he skates himself and picks his own lines. Press ⌘⌥S and the keyboard is yours.

FAQ

Does DeskSkate need any permissions?

No. Window positions come from the window server, which any app can read. DeskSkate never reads window titles or what's inside them.

Why is it asking to control Finder?

Only if you turn on Skate on Desktop Icons in the menu. Finder is the one place that knows where your desktop icons sit, so macOS asks you once. Leave it off and nothing is asked.

How do I quit it?

Click the skateboard in the menu bar and choose Quit DeskSkate. If you just want him gone for a bit, ⌃⌥H hides him.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon on macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, with no account and no ads.

Give your windows something to do

Free, under a minute to set up, and he'll be on the nearest window before you've opened anything.
